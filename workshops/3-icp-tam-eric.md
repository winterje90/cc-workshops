# Workshop 3 (Eric): ICP Pattern Mining → TAM Lists + LinkedIn Capacity Playbook

## Who it's for
Founders, growth leads, outbound owners, and ops folks responsible for list quality and channel strategy.

## Learning goals
By the end, participants can:
- Start from customer reality: enrich customer data and find patterns that define ICP.
- Validate hypotheses by checking them against the customer set.
- Generate a strong prospect list (not necessarily the whole TAM) and enrich it.
- Apply a practical rule: LinkedIn has limited daily capacity, so use it on the best leads.

## Pre-work
Bring one of:
- "Do not contact" list (proxy for customer list) or customer export.

And:
- Have a workspace set up (VS Code/Cursor + Claude Code, Node, Git).

## 90-minute agenda

**0–10 min — Why list-building is the bottleneck**
- The goal is to make list quality repeatable, not dependent on one "best list builder."

**10–25 min — Customer enrichment → ICP patterns**
- Enrich the customer list (Clay + other enrichment sources).
- If there's a hypothesis ("best customers have X"), test it on the customer set first.

**25–45 min — Live workflow: build the ICP pattern report**
Deliverables:
- ICP patterns summary (what traits show up in best customers)
- A reusable "ICP filter checklist" for prospecting

**45–60 min — Live workflow: ICP → prospect search + enrichment**
- Query a dataset to generate a list, then enrich it.
- Keep it realistic: "rock solid list" over "entire TAM."

**60–75 min — LinkedIn vs email: don't mirror sequences**
- Email volume >> LinkedIn message limits (e.g., ~20/day), so don't treat them as the same channel.
- Use LinkedIn on the absolute best leads.

**75–85 min — The LinkedIn "capacity ladder" for finding the best leads**
Use your limited LinkedIn messages on:
1. High-intent visitors (de-anon tools) → reach out normally, don't say "I saw you were on our site."
2. People engaging with your case study customer posts → name drop, don't mention the engagement.
3. People engaging with competitor content who match your ICP → use remaining capacity.

**85–90 min — Wrap: what to operationalize next week**
- One repeatable list workflow.
- One channel rule for LinkedIn capacity.

## Hands-on activities
- Enrich a customer/do-not-contact list and extract ICP patterns.
- Generate a small target list from those patterns and enrich it.
- Draft a "best leads get LinkedIn" plan based on daily capacity.

## Key takeaways
- Build ICP from customer data, then expand outward.
- Don't waste scarce LinkedIn shots on average leads.
- The goal is a list you can trust and work, not a bloated TAM file.
